Peek Demo

The Peek example, running in your browser

  1. Run a scenario in the example app: a real call through Dio, Talker, Chopper or http.
  2. Watch it arrive in Peek, the moment it is made.
  3. Open the call to read its headers, body and timing.

The demo could not start in this browser.

The calls go from your browser to httpbin.org and jsonplaceholder.typicode.com. Offline, “Add calls without a network” fills Peek with made-up ones.
