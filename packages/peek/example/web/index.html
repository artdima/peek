Peek Demo

Peek, running in your browser

  1. Use the app buy a pair of sneakers in the shop, or switch to the playground under the phone and run a scenario.
  2. Watch the calls arrive in Peek, the moment they are made.
  3. Open a call to read its headers, body and timing.

The demo could not start in this browser.

The shop talks to a pretend server inside the page, through Dio and Peek’s interceptor. The playground’s calls go from your browser to httpbin.org and jsonplaceholder.typicode.com.
